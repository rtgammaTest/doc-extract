ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

"Consolidated EBITDA" means, for any period, Consolidated Net Income for such
period adjusted to add back interest expense, taxes, depreciation and
amortisation, in each case to the extent deducted in computing Consolidated Net
Income.

"Consolidated Leverage Ratio" means, as of any date of determination, the ratio
of Consolidated Total Debt as of such date to Consolidated EBITDA for the four
fiscal quarter period most recently ended.

"Consolidated Interest Coverage Ratio" means, for any period, the ratio of
Consolidated EBITDA for such period to Consolidated Interest Expense for such
period.

"Consolidated Total Debt" means, as of any date, the aggregate principal amount
of all Indebtedness of the Borrower and its Restricted Subsidiaries outstanding
on such date.

ARTICLE IX. FINANCIAL COVENANTS

Section 9.1. Maximum Leverage. The Borrower shall not permit the "Consolidated
Leverage Ratio", as of the last day of each fiscal quarter, to be greater than
4.00 to 1.00.

Section 9.2. Minimum Interest Coverage. The Borrower shall not permit the
"Consolidated Interest Coverage Ratio", as of the last day of each fiscal
quarter, to be less than 2.50:1.00.

Section 9.3. Restricted Payments. The Borrower shall not make any Restricted
Payment unless, after giving pro forma effect thereto, the "Consolidated
Leverage Ratio" would not exceed 3.00 to 1.00 and no Default has occurred and is
continuing.
